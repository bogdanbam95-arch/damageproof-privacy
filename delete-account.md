# DamageProof — Account & Data Deletion

DamageProof users can request deletion of their account and associated cloud data.

## How to request account deletion

1. Send an email from the email address associated with your DamageProof account to **support@damageproof.co.uk**.
2. Use the subject line: **DamageProof Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include the email address associated with your DamageProof account.
4. We will process the request and confirm when the deletion has been completed.

## What will be deleted

Following a verified account deletion request, we will delete the user's DamageProof account and personal account information, along with cloud data associated with that account where applicable.

## Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as required for those purposes.

Evidence or records belonging to a Business organisation may also need to be retained as part of that organisation's business records, where applicable.

## Local data

Reports, PDFs, photos or other files saved locally on the user's device are controlled by the user and are not removed remotely by deleting the DamageProof account.
