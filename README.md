
# DamageProof Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 14 September 2026**

DamageProof ("DamageProof", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the DamageProof mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how DamageProof is used, we may process:

- Account information, such as your name, email address and account identifiers.
- Business and organisation information, including organisation names, membership, roles and company branding.
- Job information entered by users, such as customer or job names, addresses, notes and other job details.
- Photographs taken or selected as part of before-and-after damage documentation.
- Customer acknowledgements, notes, photo decisions and signatures recorded as part of an evidence report.
- Generated evidence reports and associated job records.
- Technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

DamageProof does not require users to provide information that is unrelated to the operation of the service.

## 2. Camera and Photos

DamageProof uses the device camera so users can create photographic evidence relating to a job.

Photographs may contain personal information depending on what the user chooses to photograph. Users are responsible for using the camera lawfully and appropriately and for avoiding unnecessary capture of people or personal information.

Depending on the account type and features being used, photographs and related job information may remain on the device or may be processed for the creation and storage of evidence reports.

## 3. Signatures and Customer Information

DamageProof allows users to record customer acknowledgements, notes, photo decisions and signatures.

This information is collected for the purpose of creating a record of the job and the evidence available at the time. Users and organisations using DamageProof are responsible for ensuring that customer information is collected and used appropriately in connection with their work.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information processed through DamageProof to:

- Provide and operate the DamageProof service.
- Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
- Create and manage evidence records and PDF reports.
- Provide cloud evidence features where applicable.
- Support organisation, crew and account functionality.
- Maintain security and prevent unauthorised access.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
- Respond to support, privacy and account requests.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Local and Cloud Storage

Some DamageProof information may be stored locally on the user's device.

Where cloud functionality is used, relevant account, organisation, job and evidence information may be securely transmitted to and stored using our cloud service providers.

Business organisations may make evidence available to authorised organisation members according to their assigned permissions.

Access controls are used to restrict cloud evidence to authorised accounts and organisation members.

## 6. Service Providers

DamageProof uses trusted service providers to operate parts of the service.

These may include providers of:

- Authentication and database services.
- Secure cloud storage.
- Email delivery.
- Application distribution and subscription/payment services.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own security and privacy obligations.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain evidence requested by users or organisations, meet security and operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Locally stored reports and photographs may remain on a user's device until removed by the user.

Cloud information may be retained while an account or organisation uses the relevant service and for an appropriate period afterwards where necessary for security, backup, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and device access.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing.

UK users may have rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Requests can be made using the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## 10. Children's Privacy

DamageProof is designed as a professional evidence and job-documentation service and is not intended for use by children.

We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information directly from children.

## 11. Third-Party Information

Businesses and workers using DamageProof may record information concerning their customers or other individuals.

Where an organisation or user determines why and how such information is collected through DamageProof, that organisation or user may have its own responsibilities under applicable data-protection law.

## 12. International Processing

Some service providers used to operate DamageProof may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where applicable, appropriate safeguards are used as required by data-protection law.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DamageProof develops, legal requirements change or new functionality is introduced.

The current version will be published at this location with an updated revision date.
